We study active faults, the crust, and geohazards with fiber-optic sensing.

The {{ site.group.lab }} turns fiber-optic cables, including existing telecom lines, into dense seismic arrays with sensors every few meters over tens of kilometers. We combine these observations with applied mathematics and statistical inference to image Earth structure, follow earthquakes as they rupture, and monitor hazards at resolutions that conventional seismic networks cannot reach.
