We use fiber-optic sensing to image the lithosphere, understand earthquakes, and assess geohazards.

The {{ site.group.name }} turns fiber-optic cables, including existing telecom lines, into dense arrays with sensors every few meters over tens of kilometers. We combine these observations with applied mathematics and statistical inference to image fault zones, the Moho, and subducting slabs, to follow earthquakes as they rupture, and to measure how basins, volcanoes, and precipitation shape hazards, at resolutions that conventional seismic networks cannot reach.
